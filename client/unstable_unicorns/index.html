Rules

Goal

Be the first to collect enough unicorns in your stable: 7 unicorns in a normal game (2-5 players), 6 in a big game (6+ players). Every unicorn card counts as one.

Card types

  • Baby unicorn — every player starts with one in their stable. More babies only enter your stable through card effects, never from your hand.
  • Basic unicorn — a unicorn with no effect.
  • Magical unicorn — a unicorn with a special effect.
  • Magic — a one-time effect. It goes to the discard pile right after you play it.
  • Upgrade — a lasting bonus. It stays in a stable until sacrificed or destroyed. Unless the card says otherwise, you may put it into any player's stable.
  • Downgrade — a lasting penalty. It stays in a stable until sacrificed or destroyed. Unless the card says otherwise, you may put it into any player's stable.
  • Instant (neigh) — the only card type you can play at any time, even on another player's turn. Neighing the most recent play undoes it completely; any number of neighs can chain during a turn.

Setup

Each player picks a baby unicorn for their stable. The remaining babies form the nursery. Every player is dealt 5 cards; the rest is the draw deck, with a discard pile next to it. Discarded, sacrificed, and destroyed cards all go to the discard pile.

Turn phases

  • Beginning of turn — effects that trigger "at the beginning of your turn" fire now, then you automatically draw one card from the deck. This draw is free and does not use your action.
  • Action phase — choose one: play a unicorn, magic, upgrade, or downgrade card, or draw one additional card from the deck. Either choice spends your action for the turn.
  • End of turn — discard down to the hand limit of 7 cards.

End of the game

The first player to reach the required number of unicorns in their stable wins. If the deck runs out first, the player with the most unicorns wins. If players tie for the most unicorns, the player with the most letters in their unicorn card names wins. If that ties too, everyone loses. Ouch.

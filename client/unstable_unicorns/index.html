Rules

Goal

Be the first to collect enough unicorns in your stable: 7 unicorns in a normal game (2-5 players), 6 in a big game (6+ players). Official rules.

Card types

  • Baby unicorn — Every player starts with one in their stable. More babies can only enter your stable through card effects.
  • Basic unicorn — A unicorn with no effect.
  • Magical unicorn — A unicorn with a special effect.
  • Magic — A one-time effect. It goes to the discard pile right after you play it.
  • Upgrade — A lasting bonus, active when in a stable until sacrificed or destroyed. Unless the card says otherwise, you may put it into any player's stable.
  • Downgrade — A lasting penalty. active when in a stable until sacrificed or destroyed. Unless the card says otherwise, you may put it into any player's stable.
  • Instant (neigh) — The only card type you can play at any time, even on another player's turn. Neighing the most recent play undoes it completely, and yes, you can also chain neighs.

Setup

Each player picks a baby unicorn for their stable. The remaining babies form the nursery. Every player is dealt 5 cards; the rest is the draw deck, with a discard pile next to it. Discarded, sacrificed, and destroyed cards all go to the discard pile.

Turn phases

  • Beginning of turn — Click the deck to draw a card and begin your turn. Effects that trigger "at the beginning of your turn" fire. This draw is free and does not use your action.
  • Action phase — Choose one: play a unicorn, magic, upgrade, or downgrade card, or draw one additional card from the deck. If you have multiple actions (due to an upgrade), you may perform additional actions. Note neighs only affect the results of the current action.
  • End of turn — Discard down to the hand limit of 7 cards.

End of the game

The first player to reach the required number of unicorns in their stable wins. If the deck runs out first, the player with the most unicorns wins. If players tie for the most unicorns, the player with the most letters in their unicorn card names wins. If that ties too, everyone loses. Ouch.

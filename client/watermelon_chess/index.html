Rules

  1. Red moves first.
  2. On your turn, move one of your pieces along a line to a neighbouring empty point.[1]
  3. A piece is trapped when every neighbouring point is occupied.
  4. A trapped piece is captured only if its owner has no move that can free it. A move frees the piece only when one of the pieces beside it moves away to an empty point. Capturing an attacker does not free a piece, because the attacker can answer by capturing it.
  5. Only one piece can be captured per turn. If your move traps several pieces, you pick which one is removed.
  6. You win by reducing your opponent to two pieces.

Games with no progress are drawn. This happens when a position repeats three times, or when the player to move has no legal move.

[1] Board and movement taken from Wikipedia

Controls

  • Click one of your pieces, then click a dotted point to move it there. The move is sent right away.
  • If your move traps several pieces, click the ringed piece to choose which one is removed.
  • Press Escape or click the piece again to cancel.
  • The red square marks where the last move ended.

About

This web game made by Bowserinator.

Watermelon chess (西瓜棋, Xi Gua Qi) is a traditional two player game of surrounding, played for centuries in eastern China. It is played on a round board that looks like a sliced watermelon, hence the name.

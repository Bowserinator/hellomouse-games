Rules

  • The goal of this game is to rid yourself of all your cards.
  • The first player must place down an ace (however many they want, not necessarily all of the aces they have), the second a 2, and so on until you reach J (11), Q (12), K (13) and cycle back to A (1).
  • However, you don't have to place down the card listed.
  • For example, if you had to place down a 7, you could still place down a 6.
  • If you think someone is bullshitting you (Not placing down the correct card) hit the Call BS button.
  • If you're right they get all the face down cards added to their deck.
  • However, if you're wrong you get all the face down cards added.

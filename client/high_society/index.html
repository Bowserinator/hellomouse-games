High Society

The guest list

    The house rules

    3–5 guests.

    The evening's record

      You can’t outbid. You will pass automatically.

      The Art of High Society

      • Each guest begins with 80K: money cards of 1–10K and 25K.
      • Keep your remaining money secret.

      Luxury & prestige

      • On your turn, add one or more money cards to your bid, or pass.
      • Your total must exceed the highest bid.
      • You cannot exchange cards already on the table.
      • Passing returns your bid to your hand and takes you out of this auction.
      • The last guest wins, pays their bid, and starts the next auction, and yes, a card can still be won for free.

      Disgrace

      • Bid to avoid the card.
      • The first guest to pass takes the disgrace and gets their bid back.
      • Everyone else pays their bids.
      • The taker starts the next auction.
      • Faux Pas discards one of your luxuries, or your next luxury if you have none.
      • Passé subtracts 5 status.
      • Scandale halves your final status.

      The end of the evening

      • The fourth dark-green card ends the game immediately.
      • Nobody receives it.
      • Guests tied for the least remaining money are cast out; if everyone has the same money, nobody is cast out.
      • Status = (luxury total − Passé 5) × 2 for each prestige, then ÷ 2 for Scandale.
      • Fractions round toward zero.
      • The highest status wins; ties use remaining money, then the highest luxury card.
      • A remaining tie is a shared victory.
      • Keyboard: Space or Enter selects a money card. B bids. P passes. +/− zoom. 0 recenters the table.
      Unofficial fan implementation of Reiner Knizia's High Society.

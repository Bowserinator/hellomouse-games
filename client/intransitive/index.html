Rules

  1. Blue moves first.
  2. On your turn, move one of your pieces one square in any direction, like a king in chess.
  3. You capture an enemy piece by moving onto its square when your piece beats it: rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock.
  4. You cannot move onto a piece that beats you, and you cannot move onto your own pieces.
  5. You win by moving any of your pieces onto the enemy corner, or by capturing every enemy piece.
  6. The game is drawn if the player to move has no legal move, or if 100 moves pass without a capture.

Controls

  • Click one of your pieces, then click a dotted square to move it there.
  • Press Escape or click the piece again to cancel.
  • In blitz mode each move adds 5 seconds to your own clock.

    Rules & cards

    • Play one card, then draw another. The total cannot exceed 99; negative values cannot take the total below zero
    • If no card is legal at the start of your turn, reveal your hand and lose a token. Zero tokens means you are out. The last player with tokens wins.
    • After each round, the dealer rotates clockwise
    • Reverse and skip give you another turn when only two players remain.
    • The draw pile recycles the discard pile except its top card.
    • A round with 300 plays is voided without penalty

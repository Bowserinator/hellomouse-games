Rules

Rules:
  • For 2-8 players. Each player starts with $1,500.
  • Passing or landing on Go earns you $200 and gives you the option to gamble all your money in the stock market spinner, or buy something from the black market. You may do both after passing Go.
  • Roll two dice; doubles earn you a bonus roll; three doubles in one turn sends you to Hell.
  • When you land on an unowned property, you can buy it. If you skip, a timed auction opens to all players. Bids do not extend the timer; the highest bid wins. Once you bid you cannot abstain. The auction ends early when everyone but one player has abstained.
  • You pay rent when you land on an unmortgaged deed. Bare streets in a complete set pay double; rent depends on how many s that player owns; rent is a multiple of a dice roll.
  • Build and sell evenly, and sell all buildings before mortgaging. The number of houses and hotels is limited (host adjustable).
  • You can trade cash, deeds, and "Get out of Hell" cards. When you receive a mortgaged deed in a trade, you pay 10% interest to keep it mortgaged, or principal plus interest to unmortgage it.
  • When you go bankrupt, a GoFundMe starts where other players can send you money to stay in the game. If you go bankrupt anyway, donations are refunded.
  • By default, whoever has the highest net worth after 60 minutes, or is the last player not bankrupt, wins.
Notes:
  • Landing exactly on Go or Free Parking (not just passing) lets you select a deed to auction. You may not auction deeds that would complete a set for a player.
  • In Hell, land on the exit gate, use a "Get out of Hell" card, or pay bail to leave. Bail starts at 50% of your cash and drops 10 percentage points per lap to 20%.
  • Devil's Deal: if it sends you to Hell when you pass Go, you do not collect the Go salary.
  • You may hold up to three Black Market upgrades at a time (no duplicates).
    • Dead Hand: on bankruptcy, you explode, destroying the square you're on and bankrupting other players on it. A destroyed square loses its buildings and can no longer be owned, bought, auctioned, or charged rent.

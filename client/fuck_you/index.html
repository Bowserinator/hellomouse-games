    Fuck You (aka Uno) rules

    The basics

    2-8 players. Deal 7 cards each. The first player to empty their hand after resolving effects wins. Start on a number card, then each player plays a card to match the active color, number, or symbol. Purple and ultimate cards are wild: choose a color when playing them. There is no stacking or jumping in.

    If you can't play anything, draw one card. If it is playable, play that card or pass; otherwise your turn ends. Other notes:

    • Draw two and draw four make the next player draw and miss their turn.
    • Reverse acts as Skip with two players.
    • Draw four is only legal if you have no card of the active color<

    Tick “Call UNO with my play” before playing down to one card, or press UNO afterward. Another player can catch a missed call for 2 cards until the next player acts. Effects resolve before a winner is declared, except Sacrifice explicitly wins when played last.

Controls

            
    
                
  • WASD - Move tank
    • 
                
  • Ctrl (press) - Dash in the movement direction; 3-second cooldown
    • 
                
  • Q/E (hold) - Turn tank and camera left/right
    • 
                
  • Left Click (hold) - Fire gun
    • 
                
  • Space - Leap; Shift - Ground pound while airborne
    • 
                
  • Scroll - Zoom
    • 
                
  • A/D or arrows - Cycle spectator target; Tab - Overview; drag to orbit
    • 
                
  • Mouse - Aim
    • 
            


            
Gameplay

            
    
                
  • Goal is to get as many kills as possible (Suicides and falls give no kill credit)
    • 
                
  • Touch a launch star to fly along its dotted path. Its color identifies the destination planet
    • 
                
  • Leap across holes and low obstacles. Walk into a hole and you fall out of the round
    • 
                
  • Spawn protection lasts 1.5 seconds; flight landing protection lasts 0.9 seconds. You cannot fire while blinking
    • 
                
  • Death explosions are visual only and cause no damage
    • 
                
  • Rounds end when one or fewer players are left
    • 
                
  • You get an extra point for surviving to the end of the round
    • 
                
  • Pick up powerups to get special shots / abilities. Each weapon pickup grants one shot
    • 
                
  • Powerups spawn randomly in pairs every 20 seconds, up to 8 items on the map
    • 
                
  • Bullets are destroyed when they hit another bullet (with some exceptions)
    • 
                
  • All bullet-type powerups conflict, and shield + stealth conflict. Getting a new conflicting powerup will replace the old one.
    • 
            


            
Powerups

            
    
                
  • 
                    
                    Bomb - Explodes into 25 shrapnel bullets when hit or after a delay. Its blast causes damage
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
                    Railgun - Hypersonic railgun pellet. Moves very fast, doesn't bounce
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
                    Laser - Fires instantly along the planet surface and reflects off obstacles and shields
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
                    Magnetic Mine - Launches forward, arms after 2s, then seeks the nearest tank and explodes. Cannot be shot once armed
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
                    Rocket Artillery - Calls down three rockets around the nearest player. Impact zones are marked
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
                    Shotgun - Short range burst of many small bullets
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
                    Shield - Protects you for 6s, reflecting lasers and non-rail bullets. You can still fire
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
                    Stealth - Makes you invisible to other players and any targeting (ie rocket artillery + magnetic mine)
                
    • 
                
  • 
                    
                    Teleswap - Swaps you with the nearest non-hidden player. Grants no protection after the swap

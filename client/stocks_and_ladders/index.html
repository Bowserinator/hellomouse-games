        Rules

        1. Everyone starts with $10,000. After the last round, the biggest balance wins.
        2. Each round is one trading day. Drag the four dividers on the left bar to split your whole balance across the five stocks. The split always totals 100%.
        3. Press Ready when you are done. If the timer runs out, your current split is locked in for you. You can press Unready to change your mind until then.
        4. When the day starts, your money becomes shares at the opening price. When it ends, the shares are sold at the closing price. Prices carry over into the next day.
        5. Each stock has a hidden personality, and tickers are random every match. One climbs slowly, one swings wildly, one moves in waves, one is calm until a sudden crash or spike, and one rises then falls. Watch the charts to work out who is who.
        6. Rivals' splits stay secret during the match. You only see their scores. Allocations by round are revealed at the end.
        7. While planning, scroll the chart left to look at earlier days.
